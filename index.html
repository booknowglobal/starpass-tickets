How StarPass booking works

Your request is not a ticket reservation or purchase. We confirm the details with you first.

01 — REQUEST

Choose an event

Tell us the performer, city, date preference, ticket type and quantity.

02 — CHECK

We verify details

Our team checks event status, authorized inventory, ticket availability and final pricing.

03 — PAYMENT

Receive instructions

If tickets can be supplied, we email the confirmed details and payment instructions. Do not pay before receiving them.

04 — CONFIRM

Get your ticket

After payment and ticket validity are manually verified, we send the confirmed ticket or organizer-issued entry credential.

About StarPass

A clearer way to plan your next experience.

StarPass helps customers enquire about live entertainment in selected international markets. We aim to make the request process straightforward, with event details, ticket availability and pricing confirmed before payment.

StarPass is not presented here as an official partner of any performer, venue, event organizer or ticket marketplace. Any seller authorization and inventory must be established separately and accurately.

Booking with confidence

Clear pricing: final ticket price and any applicable fees are shared before payment.

Manual payment review: no entry code is sent based solely on a payment screenshot or customer claim.

Valid entry credential: tickets and gate credentials must come from confirmed valid inventory or the event organizer's approved ticketing system.
